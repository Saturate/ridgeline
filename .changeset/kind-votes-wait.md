#### Sent back: PRs waiting on the author after your review

A new "Sent back by you" tab filter picks out PRs where your own vote is
Waiting for author or Rejected. Reviewing hides them by default and a new
Sent back tab lists them, so Reviewing only shows reviews still waiting on
you. Someone else's Waiting for author vote doesn't count.

New configs get both. Existing configs keep their saved tabs: set
Reviewing's "Sent back by you" to Hide under Settings → Tabs, and add a tab
with it set to Only.
