#### Reviewing can hide PRs you've already approved

A new "PRs you approved" tab filter leaves out PRs where your own vote is
Approved or Approved with suggestions, so Reviewing only lists reviews
still waiting on you. A team approving on your behalf doesn't count.

It's on by default in new configs. Existing configs keep their saved tabs:
turn it on under Settings → Tabs → Reviewing.
