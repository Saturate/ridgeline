#### More tab filters

Tabs can now filter by repo or provider, by author (handy for hiding
Renovate or Dependabot), by whether you're assigned directly or through a
team, by whether you're a required reviewer, by age, and by target branch.
Text filters take comma-separated patterns; a leading `!` excludes. The
README has the full list and a TOML example.
